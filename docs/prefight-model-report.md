# Prefight model: training report

Generated by `python -m src.ufc_predictor.train_prefight`. Every fight is described by what was known before it.

## Data

- 9,015 past fights in `event_cards.csv`; 8,828 have a winner and both fighters identified, and are used.
- 74% of those fights have striking and takedown history for both fighters from earlier fights (81% of the test fights).
- Fighter A and B are drawn at random per fight (share of fights where A won: 50.0%).
- No year or month feature, no corner, no career total from today.

## How it was decided and tested

| Block | Fights | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decisions (settings, kind of model, calibration), each year predicted from the years before it | 4,561 | 2014-01-04 | 2023-07-01 |
| Test, six months at a time, each predicted from everything before it | 1,770 | 2023-07-08 | 2026-11-21 |

The saved model was then trained on all 8,828 fights, up to 2026-11-21: the procedure that was tested, one step further.

The test block was also looked at for earlier versions of this model and while planning this one, so it is not untouched. What was decided (the average, the settings, the calibration) was decided on the earlier block; the live record on the site is the stricter test.
