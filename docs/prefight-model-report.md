# Prefight model: training report

Generated by `python -m src.ufc_predictor.train_prefight`. Every fight is described by what was known before it; the deployed model is not touched.

## Data

- 8,977 past fights in `event_cards.csv`; 8,767 have a winner and both fighters identified, and are used.
- 74% of those fights have striking and takedown history for both fighters from earlier fights (81% of the test fights).
- Fighter A and B are drawn at random per fight (share of fights where A won: 50.1%).
- No year or month feature, no corner, no career total from today.
